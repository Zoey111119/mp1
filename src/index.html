Welcome to Hogwarts

An orphaned boy discovers he is a wizard on his eleventh birthday, attends Hogwarts, and eventually defeats the Dark Lord Voldemort alongside his friends through the power of courage, friendship, and love.

Harry Potter Intro

Main Characters

Harry Potter

Boy Who Lived, marked by destiny and known for his courage.

Hermione Granger

The brightest witch of her age, logical, brilliant, and fiercely loyal.

Ron Weasley

A loyal friend with a brave heart and unmatched skill at Wizard's Chess.

The 7 Books

Book 1

Harry Potter and the Philosopher's Stone

Harry discovers his magical heritage on his 11th birthday and attends Hogwarts, where he uncovers the secret of the Sorcerer's Stone.

Book 2

Harry Potter and the Chamber of Secrets

Mysterious attacks paralyze Hogwarts students, leading Harry to discover a dark secret hidden deep within the Chamber of Secrets.

Book 3

Harry Potter and the Prisoner of Azkaban

Convicted murderer Sirius Black escapes Azkaban. Harry uncovers the truth about his parents' past and his connection to Sirius.

Book 4

Harry Potter and the Goblet of Fire

Harry is unexpectedly entered into the dangerous Triwizard Tournament, culminating in the harrowing return of Lord Voldemort.

Book 5

Harry Potter and the Order of the Phoenix

Facing disbelief from the Ministry of Magic, Harry and his friends form Dumbledore's Army to prepare for the coming war.

Book 6

Harry Potter and the Half-Blood Prince

Harry learns about Voldemort's past and the dark secret of Horcruxes while guided by an old textbook belonging to the Half-Blood Prince.

Book 7

Harry Potter and the Deathly Hallows

Harry, Ron, and Hermione hunt for Voldemort's remaining Horcruxes, leading to the ultimate battle at Hogwarts for the wizarding world.
